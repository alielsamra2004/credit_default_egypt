# Summary Statistics — Egyptian Retail Credit Dataset

## Overall Dataset
- **Records:** 120,000 loans
- **Period:** 2008–2024
- **Default rate:** 17.9%

## Era-Level Breakdown

| Era | Period | Records | Default Rate |
|-----|--------|---------|-------------|
| Era 1 | 2008–2010 | 23,270 | 14.9% |
| Era 2 | 2011–2014 | 22,486 | 26.5% |
| Era 3 | 2015–2019 | 36,179 | 14.4% |
| Era 4 | 2020–2024 | 28,661 | 16.4% |

## Key Findings

- **DTI_Ratio** is a direct parent of Default in all 4 eras (strongest structural predictor)
- **BN AUC by era:** 0.632, 0.664, 0.709, 0.735 (improving over time as data infrastructure matures)
- **Informal sector lift:** 2.33× among defaulters
